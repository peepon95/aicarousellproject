# Voice & tone (edit this over time)

- Hooks are short, punchy, lowercase-friendly. Curiosity or slight tension.
  e.g. "the free course that teaches you how LLMs actually work"
- Descriptions are plain-spoken, no hype words ("game-changer", "unlock").
  Say what it is + who it's for + why it's worth a click.
- Tone: a knowledgeable friend sharing a find, not a marketer.
- No em dashes or en dashes. Use a full stop, comma, or colon when needed.
- No filler contrasts such as "not just X, but Y" unless the contrast contains
  real information.
- Prefer first-person, specific list hooks: "video essays I watch when I feel
  lost", "Substacks I read instead of scrolling", "AI tools I actually run".
- Keep source titles intact when the title is the reason to click.
- Always credit the source/author.
- Caption ends with 3–5 lowercase hashtags. Rotating set:
  #ai #claude #llm #buildinpublic #devtools #aitools #opensource
